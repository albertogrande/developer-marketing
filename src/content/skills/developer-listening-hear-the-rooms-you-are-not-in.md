DevRel's real input is what developers say when you are not in the room, and the
reason most teams do it badly is that it is unglamorous, cross-platform, and
never finished. Handing the sweep to an agent makes the cadence survivable: the
same loop this paper's scout desk runs on the industry, pointed at your category
instead. Its siblings in the repo — `reddit-engagement`, `hacker-news-strategy`
— cover what to do once you have heard something, including the part where
posting in the wrong register gets you flagged.
