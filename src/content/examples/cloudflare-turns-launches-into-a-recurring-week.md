Instead of scattering releases across the calendar, Cloudflare batches them into
recurring themed weeks — Developer Week, Birthday Week, Security Week — each a
daily drop of real product, mostly free tier. The rhythm does the marketing:
developers learn the dates, the press pre-books coverage, and every day compounds
the last. It's the runnable-launch play at an operating cadence — the 2024
inference launch during one such week drove a reported ~15% spike in new
developer signups. The format is the moat; a single big-bang launch can't create
the same standing anticipation.
