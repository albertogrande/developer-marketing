---
title: Localize the checkout before you localize the marketing
when: Planning international growth for a developer-facing or AI product, or prioritizing the billing roadmap against new-market campaigns.
do: Add local payment methods and adaptive local-currency pricing in your top non-home markets before spending on country-specific marketing, and measure conversion by market and payment method against the home-market baseline.
why: Stripe's transaction data across 35 markets shows the 100 largest AI companies sell into ~120 markets by year three and take 48% of revenue outside their home market. Adding local payment methods lifted conversion 7.4% and revenue 12% on average; adaptive pricing lifted initial conversion 4.7% and subscription lifetime value 5.4%; and the fastest-growing cohort uses 2x more local payment methods than average. The checkout is the cheaper, measured lever — translated campaigns are neither.
section: 06-channels-and-distribution
tags: [pricing, metrics, distribution]
since: Stripe, "Mapping the AI economy" (2026-08-12) — transaction data covering 88% of the 2026 Forbes AI 50
verify: Re-read the Stripe report for the current figures, then check your own non-home conversion rate by payment method; the claim weakens if your data shows no gap.
