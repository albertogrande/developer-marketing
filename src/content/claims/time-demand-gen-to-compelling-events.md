title: Time demand-gen to compelling events, not to your launch calendar
when: Planning outbound or campaign timing for a devtool whose buyers face platform-forced work — SDK deprecations, breaking major versions, policy deadlines.
do: Build the campaign calendar around the moments a developer must touch the integration anyway, and staff free migration help — including for non-customers — ahead of the deadline, so the trust exists before the next forced moment.
why: Rik Haandrikman (VP of Growth, RevenueCat) argues the "compelling event" — his example, Google Play Billing Library major-version deprecations — is the one moment a developer will voluntarily touch billing code, and says RevenueCat's free migration support for non-customers converted "a bunch" of them at the next forced migration. Self-reported and anecdotal, but a named operator describing a repeatable mechanism, and it inverts the deprecation ledger this site keeps — another vendor's sunset is your channel.
