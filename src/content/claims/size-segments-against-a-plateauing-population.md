---
title: Size your market against a plateauing developer population
when: Sizing a market, setting awareness targets, or choosing between broad reach and segment depth in a developer GTM plan.
do: Plan against a plateau, not a boom — pick a specific developer segment and go deep rather than spreading spend across "all developers." Re-check the population figures against the latest SlashData wave before quoting them.
why: SlashData's 2026 research puts the global developer population at roughly 47M with growth decelerating to about 10% year over year, aging, and shifting toward South Asia and Greater China. Broad-awareness plays priced for a fast-growing pool stop paying back when the pool flattens; depth in a well-chosen segment compounds instead.

The plateau is the dated fact; "pick a segment" is the old advice it re-prices. A bare model will still quote population figures and growth rates from earlier waves — carry the current wave's numbers and the deceleration, not just the headcount.
