title: Target buyer-shaped queries, not high-volume explainer keywords
when: Planning technical content for discovery — deciding which search or assistant queries a piece should win.
do: Aim new content at middle- and bottom-of-funnel questions phrased the way a buyer would ask an assistant ("X vs Y for this workload", "how to migrate from X"), and stop commissioning high-volume, low-intent explainers ("what is a data warehouse").
why: Karl Hughes (Draft.dev) reportedly told Scaling DevTools that client traffic on low-intent explainer content dipped through 2025 as AI-summarized search absorbed those queries zero-click, pushing content ROI toward comparison-shaped, purchase-adjacent questions — a single-sourced, agency-observed pattern across devtools clients that fits the broader zero-click trend on definitional queries but isn't independently confirmed here.
