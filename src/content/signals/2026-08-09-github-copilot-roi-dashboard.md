This is the dashboard's second dated move in three weeks: the named-"Passive"
seat segment landed 07-22, and this adds the four-number shape (cost/dev,
%-of-payroll, PRs/dev, adoption phase) the CFO dive argued a program should be
reported in. The cohort split is GitHub's own adoption-phase taxonomy, not an
independent measure of output quality.
