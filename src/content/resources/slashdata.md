The default source when someone asks how many developers there are in a segment and you need an answer with a method behind it. Population sizing and programme benchmarking are the two products that change decisions: the first stops you building a business case on a market that does not exist at that size, the second tells you whether your developer programme is average or actually good. Expect analyst-firm pricing. Read the methodology first, and treat every headline population figure, including theirs, as an estimate with a confidence interval attached.
