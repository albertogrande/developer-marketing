The structural answer to the main complaint about DevRel agencies, which is that you meet the senior person in the pitch and then work with someone else. Here there is no intermediary to hide behind: you contract the individual, at their rate, for the specific slice of work you need, whether that is a launch, a docs overhaul or covering a parental leave. That also means no account manager chasing deliverables, so it suits a buyer who can brief clearly and manage a contractor. Worth watching as it fills out.
