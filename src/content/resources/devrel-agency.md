Their signature engagement is a developer-experience friction audit: someone competent walks your onboarding as a stranger would and writes down every place it hurts. That is the highest-value week of consulting most devtools can buy, because the findings are specific, cheap to fix and impossible to see from the inside. The public artifacts, the book, the journey map, the annual report, are how you check the thinking before you spend. Buy the audit first and decide about a retainer afterwards.
