Developer influencer marketing is a real channel now, and this is the operator layer on top of it: brief once, and they handle creator selection, production and the paid distribution behind it. The reason to use a broker rather than emailing YouTubers yourself is the boring part, contracts, briefs and consistent measurement across a dozen creators. The reason to be careful is that the audience punishes an ad that pretends not to be one harder than any other channel. Insist on visible disclosure and on creators who would use the product anyway.
