Where to go when the product is understood internally and nowhere else. The work is messaging and narrative for companies whose technology genuinely is complicated, and the deliverable is a structure your own team can keep using, not a deck that ages out in a quarter. Publishing the frameworks openly is the tell: an agency confident enough to give away the method is selling the judgment, which is the part you cannot copy. Engage them before you commission a content programme, not after it underperforms.
