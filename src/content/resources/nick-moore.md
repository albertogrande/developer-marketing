The counter-argument to hiring an agency at all. For a team publishing two or three considered pieces a month, a single senior writer who learns your product properly beats a rotating cast of contractors, and the price of the two is closer than agencies would like you to notice. Essays and bottom-of-funnel comparisons are the strength here rather than volume tutorials. If your programme needs twelve pieces a month, this is the wrong shape and an agency is the honest answer.
