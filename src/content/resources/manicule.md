The interesting bet here is the division of labour: agents run the mechanical passes, verifying every code sample against the live API, auditing links and coverage, while people decide what the docs should say and how they should be structured. That is roughly the right split, and it is the reason they can rebuild a documentation set in weeks rather than quarters. Treat the youth as the real risk and the speed as the real product. For a startup whose docs are three months behind the API, that trade is often worth making.
