Matthew Revell has been arguing about what DevRel is for longer than most companies have had a DevRel team, and Hoopy sells that accumulated judgment as audits, strategy and training. The qualitative research offer is the underrated one: actually interviewing your developers about why they stalled, rather than inferring it from a funnel chart. Take them when the question is "is our developer programme working and what should it be", and when the answer needs to survive contact with an exec team. Not the shop for staffing a conference booth next month.
