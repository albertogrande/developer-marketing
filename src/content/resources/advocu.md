At a certain size a champions programme stops being a spreadsheet and starts being an operations problem: applications to triage, activity to track, contributions to reward, and an exec asking what any of it produced. This is the tooling for that, with people attached who have watched other companies do it. Relevant once you have more than a handful of advocates and no idea who is actually active. Premature if you are still deciding whether the programme should exist, which is a strategy question you should answer first.
