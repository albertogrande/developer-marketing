The buy-a-programme option for hackathons and developer events, at a scale no in-house team is going to replicate. What you are actually purchasing is repetition in front of people who will be making build-versus-buy decisions in three years, which is a legitimate strategy and a terrible one to justify on a quarterly dashboard. Sponsor it if your tool is free to start, genuinely fun to build with, and you can be patient. Do not sponsor it expecting attributable revenue.
