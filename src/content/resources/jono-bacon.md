The reference point for building a community deliberately instead of hoping one forms around a Discord invite. The engagements are strategy and coaching: what the community is for, how contribution ladders work, what to measure, and how to keep the thing alive when the launch enthusiasm fades. Useful when leadership has agreed to invest in community and nobody can articulate what success looks like. Less useful if what you actually need is someone to answer forum posts on Tuesday.
