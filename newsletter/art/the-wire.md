# The Wire — ASCII art

Cover masthead and author portraits for the newsletter, in plain text —
the only image format this publication will ever ship. Everything here is
hand-drawn ASCII: paste it into an `.eml`, a terminal, or a `<pre>` block
and it renders everywhere, tracks nothing, and weighs nothing.

The five faces are the writing desks from [AUTHORS.md](../../AUTHORS.md).
