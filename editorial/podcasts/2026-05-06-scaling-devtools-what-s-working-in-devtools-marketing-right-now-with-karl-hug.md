## What it covers

Karl Hughes presents results from Draft.dev's first formal devtools-marketing
survey (~50 respondents, collected in person at conferences plus sent to
clients/prospects, closed ~November–December 2025): 2026 budget trends, how
teams are actually using AI in marketing, which channels deliver ROI, and why
SEO content built around high-volume/low-intent keywords ("what is a data
warehouse") is losing traffic to zero-click AI answers.

## Claims worth checking

- [01:38–03:13] 62% of surveyed devtools marketing teams are increasing 2026
  budgets vs. 2025; ~10% said they're likely to decrease. Self-reported
  survey, ~50 respondents, not statistically rigorous per Hughes himself — a
  directional read, not a benchmark.
- [07:11–07:42] 96% of respondents said they've tried AI tools for marketing
  work; only ~7% said it's "unlocking significant value" (saving hours
  reliably, low rework). Self-reported survey; notable gap between
  experimentation and realized value as of survey close (Nov/Dec 2025).
- [08:16–08:30] ~60% use AI most consistently for written-content
  editing/planning; adoption is bottlenecked less by model quality than by
  fragmented, unsanctioned individual workflows inside companies (no shared
  account/tooling across a marketing team). Self-reported/anecdotal.
- [21:36–22:04] Draft.dev's channel survey: influencer/video marketing has the
  *lowest* measurable ROI of any channel right now but the *highest*
  experimentation rate; in-person events/meetups rank as the top channel for
  ROI. Self-reported survey.
- [27:11–28:22] Frames the last year's SEO shift concretely: high-volume,
  low-purchase-intent explainer content ("what is a data warehouse") is
  increasingly zero-clicked by AI-summarized search results; Draft.dev clients
  saw traffic dips as this took hold through 2025, pushing content strategy
  toward middle/bottom-of-funnel, comparison-style queries closer to what a
  buyer would actually ask ChatGPT. Anecdotal/agency-observed, not measured
  with client-level numbers.
- [40:12–41:17] Draft.dev's own client-acquisition mix, stated as deliberate
  and stable across two different agencies he owns: ~30% referral, ~30%
  SEO/GEO/AEO, ~30% social/events/unattributable. Self-reported, single
  agency's mix, not a benchmark for the industry.
