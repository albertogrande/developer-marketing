## What it covers

swyx (Shawn Wang — AI Engineer conference/community, Latent Space podcast, at
the time joining Cognition) argues DevRel went quiet during the "products
alone win" PLG era and is now back, driven by platform/API plays needing
usage explanation and by cheap AI-era venture money funding experimentation.
He gives founders a playbook for "uphill DevRel" (no traction yet, no social
proof) versus "downhill DevRel" (already popular): lead with a mission
worth joining, be radically transparent about how the product works so a
sale demonstrates you solved a hard problem, and own a superlative — number
one at something provable, speed being the easiest default — rather than
vague "better DX" claims.

## Claims worth checking

- "Anthropic — $1B of revenue in Claude Code — cannot fill their Head of

  just interviewed with the hiring manager); dated December 2025, so check

- Anthropic launched an official MCP gateway around August 2025 and "no one

- General claim that DevRel engagement/hiring interest "died out" during the
  PLG-only period and picked back up "at the start of this year" (2025) —
  swyx's personal observation from founder/VC conversations, not survey data.
  [~11:07]
