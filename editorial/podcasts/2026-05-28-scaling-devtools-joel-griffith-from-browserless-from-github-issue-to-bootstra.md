## What it covers

Joel Griffith on bootstrapping Browserless (headless-browser infrastructure)
from a GitHub issue thread to a profitable, fully bootstrapped ~10-person
company over eight years. Covers finding the first paying customer inside a
support thread, why a single early "lessons learned" blog post became the
company's top-performing content for years, why cold outbound has underperformed
for them, and how agent/browser-automation demand has roughly doubled signups
since late 2025.

## Claims worth checking

- [11:43–14:26] First paid user came directly from answering the most-commented
  open GitHub issue on Puppeteer (how to run Chrome headless on
  Ubuntu/Docker) with a working Dockerfile, then pointing frustrated
  commenters at a hosted version. Self-reported origin story, but a concrete,
  copyable channel: mine your dependency's own issue tracker for the
  most-repeated unsolved problem.
- [16:45–17:42] A single long-form "here's everything that worked and didn't"
  post written ~6–12 months in hit #1 on Hacker News and, per Griffith, is
  "still probably one of our top performing blog posts" years later.
  Self-reported, no traffic numbers given.
- [32:02–32:26] Sales motion is "a lot of product-led growth" — self-serve
  signups escalate to sales already 2-3 steps into the funnel; cold outbound
  yielded "a fraction of a percent" click-through/response across "thousands"
  of emails sent. Self-reported, no absolute numbers, but a clear, specific
  outbound-vs-inbound verdict from a bootstrapped devtool with no CMO
  spinning the story.
- [38:26–38:46] Daily signups have "about doubled" since late 2025, attributed
  to agents/AI coding tools driving browser-automation demand. Self-reported,
  directional only — no baseline numbers.
- [25:20–25:38] Frames long-form technical content as doing double duty for
  AEO ("AI engine optimization") — more context and explicit Q&A framing in a
  post reportedly correlates with better AI-discoverability, in his
  experience. Anecdotal, not measured; consistent with but not independent
  confirmation of the guide's existing GEO hedge.

## Why it matters here

A clean, specific case for `07-launches` and `06-channels-and-distribution`:
an issue-tracker-as-acquisition-channel story with no marketing budget behind
it, plus a direct, first-person PLG-inbound-beats-cold-outbound verdict from a
bootstrapped (not VC-backed) devtool — useful as a counterweight to
enterprise-focused GTM advice elsewhere in the corpus. All self-reported by
the founder with no third-party numbers; treat as one practitioner's
experience, not benchmark data.
