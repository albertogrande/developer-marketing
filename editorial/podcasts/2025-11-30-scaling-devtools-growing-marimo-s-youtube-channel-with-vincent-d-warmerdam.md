## What it covers

Vincent D. Warmerdam (Marimo, a Python notebook) walks through seven months
of building the company's YouTube channel from zero: an education-first
phase (answer every FAQ as a short video, roughly the first 2–3 months),
then YouTube Shorts tied to Marimo's 2–3 releases a week, then targeted
"nerd-snipe" videos aimed at adjacent communities (e.g. a Marimo/Django
integration tutorial to reach Django users). Every video is unscripted
live-coding except the intro; consistency (one long-form video + several
shorts weekly) is his stated single biggest lever, not production value.

## Claims worth checking

- ~650K YouTube views by month 7, up from ~500K at month 6 — self-reported,
  Marimo's own analytics, not independently verified. [~00:45]
- Shorts get a minimum of ~2,000 views each, with 40–50% from viewers who'd
  never watched the channel before — self-reported, YouTube's own stats
  panel per Warmerdam. [~01:50]
- Weekly download growth "somewhere around 5–6%" — Warmerdam flags this
  himself as an estimate he hasn't rechecked against the pre-YouTube
  baseline; treat as directional, not a clean before/after number. [~08:46]
- "Roughly one in five" respondents to an in-product/Discord feedback
  questionnaire cite a YouTube video as how they found Marimo — self-reported
  survey of an unrepresentative, self-selected sample (people who bothered
  to fill in the form), not a measured attribution channel. [~07:00]

## Why it matters here

A concrete, single-company account for the **earned distribution** thread
(`editorial/MEMORY.md`) and the content/channels guide sections — a small
team (one part-time creator) treating YouTube as a second search engine and
a documentation supplement, with a stated cadence (5–7 shorts/week, one
long-form video/week) as the growth lever rather than production polish.
The "hire your DevRel, not a DevRel" framing (someone excited about the
specific product, not the category) echoes the same-week swyx episode
(`2025-12-05-scaling-devtools-devrel-is-unbelievably-back-with-swyx.md`) —
two guests converging on the same claim independently strengthens it as a
practice candidate. All figures are self-reported and unaudited; no claim
here belongs in the guide without independent confirmation.
